01

Career planning

The part no course teaches.

Career planning, not just professional development.

Professional development is the certificates. Project management, analytics, the skills you choose to learn. I value those. I used them. Career planning is the layer above: who you know, what you know, and what you need to learn next.

At Bell Labs I moved from the economic analysis group into a technology group knowing no technology. The company sent me to programming training on campus, then to a company on the West Coast for two weeks to learn object oriented programming. It changed my work.

“Intentional planning, sometimes a little serendipity, the right people around you, and training exactly when you need it.”

Experience that informs the work

Perspective earned across sectors, roles, and reinventions.

The value is not the résumé. It is the judgment and the pattern recognition that come from working through change, at personal scale and at institutional scale.

Reinventing across sectors
