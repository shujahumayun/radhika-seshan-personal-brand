01

Why Radhika

A career that crossed boundaries.

I know what it means to build—and rebuild—a career.

My own career has crossed economics, technology, product leadership, and higher education. I began as an economist, built products and led global teams in the technology industry, and later moved into academic leadership, where I have helped people and institutions respond to changing professions, technologies, and learner expectations.

That path was not linear. It required me to recognize transferable strengths, enter unfamiliar environments, make difficult choices, and continue learning. It is why I believe that a successful career is not something we discover once. It is something we learn to shape deliberately.

“The objective is not simply to feel more confident. It is to see your situation more clearly, make stronger choices, and act with greater agency.”
