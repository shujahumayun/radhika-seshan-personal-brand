01

Coaching

You know the destination. What you do not have yet is the route.

For the professional ready to take the next step.

Mid career professionals, women among them, moving up, moving sideways, or changing course. People who moved to the US for work, and people who have been here all along and are asking the same questions.

The part no course teaches is reading the room, and knowing that what worked before will not always work now. We work on both layers: the career planning above the skills, and the skills themselves.

01

Direction

We start with where you are, what you are actually reaching for, and whether that destination is the right one. Sometimes that last question is the whole first conversation.

02

Route options

I draw the route options from experience and we choose one together. Then we turn it into moves you can make this month: the conversation, the introduction, the skill, the application.

03

Accountability

After each step we ask whether it took you where you needed to go, and what to pivot. I hold you to the plan you made, and I will tell you when it stops working.

This is coaching, not counseling and not motivation. It runs as a conversation rather than a curriculum, and it is paid work with a clear scope agreed before we start.
