A real world roadmap for career development

Career planning is the layer above professional development.

You already have what it takes. The next step is a strategy for what comes next, and someone to build it with you.

Professional development is the certificates. Project management, analytics, the skills you choose to learn. I value those. I used them.

Career planning is who you know, what you know, and what you need to learn next. It is the part no course teaches, and it is where I work.

01 / Career planning

Career planning, not just professional development.

The part no course teaches.

At Bell Labs I moved from the economic analysis group into a technology group knowing no technology. The company sent me to programming training on campus, then to a company on the West Coast for two weeks to learn object oriented programming. It changed my work.

That is what career planning looks like in practice. Not a certificate collected in advance, but the right training arriving at the moment the next step actually needs it, because somebody had thought about where the step was going.
