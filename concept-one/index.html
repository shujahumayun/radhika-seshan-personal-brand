Coaching · Speaking & Advisory

Own your journey.

Striving is trying without being attached to guaranteed results. It is playing the long game against a five year vision, not the next promotion.

Positive action means not sweating what you cannot change. It is having a real world roadmap, and taking action on it.

Every step you take is one you own, in what you do and how you feel about it. That ownership is what creates agency. You know you can do it, or you know you can learn it. Either way, it is yours.

The distinction

Career development, not professional development.

A career is what you choose to do. Professional development is what you choose to learn.

Radhika built hers by doing both. A PhD economist who moved into high tech product management, she was one of ten economists in a company of a hundred thousand technologists. What she has to offer is not a credential. It is proof that a career gets built through applied learning and deliberate navigation, not the degree alone.
