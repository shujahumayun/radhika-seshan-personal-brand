The distinction

Career planning, not just professional development.

Professional development is the certificates: project management, analytics, the skills you choose to learn. Radhika values those. She used them. Career planning is the layer above: who you know, what you know, and what you need to learn next.

At Bell Labs she moved from the economic analysis group into a technology group knowing no technology. The company sent her to programming training on campus, then to a company on the West Coast for two weeks to learn object oriented programming. It changed her work. Intentional planning, sometimes a little serendipity, the right people around you, and training exactly when you need it.

Experience that informs the work
