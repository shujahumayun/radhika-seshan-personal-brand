Concept 1 · his layout, her words

A real world roadmap for career development

Kumar's mockup is the design. Everything readable on it now comes from the 26 August review call: her hero line, the credibility strip as titles rather than employers, the Bell Labs story behind career planning, and her own "own your journey" framework in her own three steps, ending on "the roadmap is what we develop together, the journey stays yours". Every figure and the current employer name have been taken out and stay out until she signs off.

  • DesignKumar's, kept. Background moved to pure white, the portrait squared with soft corners, italics removed from the copy
  • CopyHers, taken from the call notes and written in first person throughout
  • Taken outThe four figures, the University of Minnesota name, and "three decades of reinvention"
  • Still openThe hero wording, the framework and the audience count are hers to choose. This build shows one working answer to each
