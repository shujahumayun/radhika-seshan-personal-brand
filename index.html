Two homepage concepts.

Same words, same palette, two different personalities. Open each one, scroll the whole page, then tell me which structure feels like you. Everything here is a working design in the browser, not a flat picture, so buttons, hovers, and the mobile layout all behave the way the finished site will.

Concept One

Editorial and calm

A serif voice with a portrait card beside the headline. Sections arrive one clear idea at a time, and the page closes on an iron gray band. This is the quieter of the two.

  • TypeDM Serif Display headlines, Inter for everything else
  • AccentDeep violet, used only on small marks
  • HeroHeadline left, photograph in a card on the right

Concept Two

Bold and modern

A full width photograph behind the opening headline, tighter type, and a two cell story block. More presence at first glance, and the page closes on a deep violet band.

  • TypeInter Tight headlines, one serif italic word for emphasis
  • AccentPlum, used on rules, links, and the primary button
  • HeroFull width photograph with the headline over it

A few things to keep in mind while you look

  • The photographs are not you. Every image is a free stock placeholder, chosen only to hold the right shape and crop. Your own photographs replace them.
  • The words are the draft deck from our content workshop, still waiting on your sign off. Read them, but judge the layout first.
  • The email address and the LinkedIn link are placeholders.
  • Both concepts follow the rules we agreed: no testimonials, no invented numbers, no pricing, and no employer name anywhere.
