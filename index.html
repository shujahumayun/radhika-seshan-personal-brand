The concept

Radhika Seshan, career strategy

Hero leads with Kumar's line, "your experience has brought you this far, let us make it work harder for what comes next", over your portrait, with one credibility line directly beneath it as titles only. A first person passage on why the unusual career matters to a client. Three ways to work together, side by side: individuals, cohorts and groups, organizations. "Own your journey" carries Kumar's three steps and closes on "a real world roadmap, built together, the journey stays yours". Then career planning versus professional development with the Bell Labs story, three story led experience panels, a selected evidence row with the figures held back, a Perspectives section of three draft essays, a testimonials placeholder, and a closing question.
