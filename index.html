The consolidated concept

Concept, version 2

Built from the 2026-08-26 call and Kumar's forwarded analysis. Person first hero with your name set large and your photograph beside it. The tagline "A real world roadmap for career development" carries the weight, "you already have what it takes" steps back. Credibility sits directly under the hero as titles only, no employer names, no numbers. "Own your journey" is now three connected numbered blocks that end on "the roadmap is what we develop together, the journey stays yours". Three ways to work with you, side by side. A short "experience that informs the work" section told as stories, not a resume. A clearly marked placeholder where testimonials will go. One question as the closing call to action.
